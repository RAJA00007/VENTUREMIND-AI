What It Does

+

Most diligence tools summarize a deck and call it analysis. VentureMind starts from evidence: the repository, the market data, the funding record, and only reaches a verdict once every specialist agent has filed its findings.

01

Technical Diligence, Not Just Deck Review

Reads the actual repository, covering commit cadence, test coverage, tech debt, and contributor concentration, and flags AI generated boilerplate dressed up as a real product.

02

A Committee, Not a Single Score

Specialist agents each investigate a company independently. The committee publishes majority and minority views side by side, including a dedicated case for why the deal fails.

03

Every Claim Traced to Evidence

Output is confidence gated: any figure without a verifiable source is surfaced as unverified rather than quietly filled in.

How It Works

+

One prompt is enough: "Analyze this startup and tell me whether it's a good investment." From there, the analysis runs itself.

01

Start With a Name

Give VentureMind a company, a website, or a repository. No forms, no data room upload required to begin.

02

Specialists Work in Parallel

Research, code, market, competitor, founder, finance, and risk agents each investigate their own dimension independently and at the same time.

03

The Committee Synthesizes a Verdict

Findings are reconciled into one memo: a recommendation, the evidence behind it, and everywhere the agents disagreed with each other.
